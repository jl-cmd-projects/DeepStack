# Room to miss

An attempt that must not fail gets smaller until it cannot teach anything. Give the attempt room to miss, and keep the outside judge so a miss is visible.

Open [deepstack-mode](../deepstack-mode/SKILL.md) for the trigger that reaches this leaf.
