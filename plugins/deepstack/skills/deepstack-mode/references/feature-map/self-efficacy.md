# Self-efficacy

The stance that lets a person attempt a goal they had filed as impossible.

## Sub-features

- specific-hard-goal: a numbered or pass/fail target.
- seen-success: one case where the goal has already been met.
- room-to-miss: the attempt may fail and still be the right attempt.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The user calls the goal impossible.
- The user asks for a best effort with no target.

## Driving it with the DeepStack skills

Preconditions: a goal the user wants.

- Open [principle-specific-hard-goals](../../../principle-specific-hard-goals/SKILL.md). Result: a specific hard target.
- Open [self-efficacy](../../playbooks/self-efficacy.md). Result: the stance, then altitude, depth, or range, plus an outside judge.

## Gotchas

- The stance is not the proof. The trust card still has to pass.
- A vague "do your best" is a weaker goal than a specific hard one.
